Three behaviours are deliberate and can look incomplete on a short pass.

1. The introduction cannot be dismissed by dragging. It is three full-screen pages, not a stuck sheet. End it with "Continue" on "Rest seals the night", or with "Skip" on any page. "Skip" also puts page lengths back to 90, 120, and 90 seconds and turns "Haptics" on. "Next" then "Continue" leaves those settings as they are.

2. "Begin with Reflect" and "Keep this page" start a timed beat. The primary control stays dimmed while the cue cycles "Inhale", "Hold", "Exhale", "Settle". That wait is the rite, not a hang. "End early" still keeps the page. After the keep, the page does not auto-advance; tap "Turn forward".

3. "Sealed nights" opens empty until the first "Rest", showing "No sealed night yet" and "Keep the three pages, then rest. The mark will wait here." An unfinished night never appears there. "Back to tonight" dismisses the sheet.
